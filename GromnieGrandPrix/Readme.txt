GROMNIE GRAND PRIX
The Dereth Cup for Windows

Double-click Play.cmd. Choose a course, mount, weapon, and rider appearance
in the stable. Scroll the mount color row to browse the full palette for
each mount. Cow, Auroch Bull, Chicken, Bunny, Asheron (Piggyback), Grievver, Mukkir, Shreth, Mattekar,
Reedshark and Bael'Zharon (Front Carry) use native movement animations.
The Festivus Flying Carpet and Isin Dule's Mage Disc hover over the course.
The Mage Disc radiates the native Crimson Storm lightning from underneath.
Carenzi Skates put a native Carenzi Racer under each boot.
Each mount remembers its own selected color. All mounts share the same pace and handling.
Boosts play the mount's own call. Asheron has original synthesized banter.

Rivals brake for bends, look for room to pass, dodge hazards, and hold items
for useful openings. They react to repeated swipes nearby and learn from
fast, clean corners you take. Learned racing lines are saved locally per course.

WASD or arrow keys  Accelerate, brake and steer across the course
Space + steer       Start a drift and hold Space to charge, then release to boost
Q / E               Use your weapon to the left / right
C                   Time a block with your equipped shield
F                   Use the item you collected from a cycling icon
R                   Barrel roll in flight to dodge a spell
V                   Cycle chase, first person, isometric, tilt-shift and wide chase views
Esc                 Pause, open graphics/audio settings, restart or return to the stable

Ground racing cruises at 60 mph. Standard boosts can reach 80 mph, and
super drifts can reach 100 mph. Drifts trail smoke while charging, and a
super drift leaves fire on release. Set the speedometer to MPH or km/h in
the Graphics settings.

The Kaskara has good reach and takes three hits to unseat a fresh
rider. The fast Dagger takes four. The heavy Morning Star takes two.
Individual hits barely cut speed. When balance runs out the rider flies off
the mount and remounts at the last checkpoint. Get alongside a rival
before you swing. Tap accelerate just before GO for a rocket start.

COURSES

Every course uses a regional layout. Yanshi XL, Samsur XL, Glenden Wood XL,
Holtburg XL and Shoushi XL each load an 8-by-8 grid of 64 contiguous native
landblocks. Every grid measures 1,536 m by 1,536 m. The racing lines cover
6.56 to 7.33 km and cross 42 to 47 landblocks, including every row and
column. The routes wind inland past native buildings, ridges and roads.
Yanshi passes through the village, Samsur skirts the crater, and Shoushi
sweeps around the gates of the walled town.

XL races are one lap with 16 checkpoints and pickups and boost pads spaced
along the full route. The minimap shows the 8-by-8 grid behind the racing
line. Each revised XL circuit has a fresh best time. Prior XL times are
kept in the local save as legacy bests.

Search for Obsidian Plains to find Black Hills Crossing and Split Summit.
Both load an 8-by-8 native grid. Ride through the Olthoi icon in the middle
of the road to saddle up on a winged Spitter and fly from ridge to ridge.
Steer to bank through the wind rings.
The outside lanes follow the hillside on the ground route.
Both routes rejoin before the next checkpoint.

Obsidian Plains - Aerbax's Citadel adds a third flight over the native
floating platform above the Singularity Bore. Five mock players trade
spells with Aerbax. Bank around stray bolts, roll through with R, or take
the ground route.
A midair hit knocks the rider off and returns them to the last checkpoint.

Four additional XL circuits use 64 native landblocks apiece. Singularity
Caul winds between Caulcano, the Obsidian Rim and Caul Asylum. Crater Lake
passes the village, the caldera and the Stone of Snowreap on Mount Esper.
Aerlinthe Island links the slopes of Tenkarrdun with its reservoir,
anchorage and Western Lighthouse. Marae Lassel crosses the Marescent
Plateau near North Fork Dam, the Deru Trees and the Breached Hive.

Aerlinthe Grand Loop is a separate 9.71 km, one-lap race on a 14 × 14 grid.
Follow the coast past both Watchers and lighthouses, then cut inland through
the lower slopes of Tenkarrdun, Relic Town and the Aerlinthe Reservoir.

On Rynthid Singularity, the leading racer advances the battle by clearing
laps. The first racer through lap five triggers the seal while the rest
of the field keeps racing. Results appear when the field finishes or the
finish window expires. Recent lap times appear above the track, and the
results show each rider's lap splits and total.

Import from ACE brings your character's current look into the stable.
On the Server tab, enter your usual shard address and sign in. Choose a
character from the list and select Import Character. The game briefly enters
the world with that character to read the current appearance, then signs out.
Log out of the original client first. The game uses the shard's native login
protocol. The password is session-only.

Use Saved Templates to reapply an imported rider offline or save a named
copy of a custom rider. For shards that offer /gromnielook, the Pairing Code
tab also accepts the shard's appearance URL and one-time code.

Track Editor
Open Track Editor in the stable. Jump to a familiar course or type a
landblock hex ID from your DATs. Expand the terrain from 1 × 1 to 14 × 14.
Click to add spline points and drag a point to move it. Alt-click inserts
one into the nearest segment. Right-click removes a point.
Straight, sweeper and S-bend pieces snap to the grid and attach to the
last point. Q and E rotate the piece. Scroll to zoom, middle-drag to pan.
Select a point and choose Set Start to move the starting grid.
Use Close Loop or double-click the first point to join the circuit.
A piece ending at the first point also closes the loop.
The dirt road follows the grade and cross slope of the native hills.
Packed shoulders keep ground racers on the road and fade into the terrain.
Test Drive uses the current draft.
Return to the stable from a test race to go back to editing. Saved tracks
appear in the course picker and can be reopened from Saved Tracks.
On Windows, track JSON files are stored under
%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Gromnie Grand Prix\tracks.

Choose your weapon and shield in the stable. The spear reaches across
lanes, the Greataxe has a wide sweep, and the javelin and Storm Staff
fire sideways across the track. The Staff cuts an active rival boost.
The Round Shield turns a melee parry into a boost, the Tower Shield
covers attacks and obstacles, and the Prismatic Diamond Shield converts
a blocked spell into boost. Each guard stops one hit before recharging.
Finish your swing before raising the shield.

Choose Holtburg to Cragstone for the point-to-point Obsidian Span sprint.
Follow the native road out of Holtburg, cross both stone spans, then take
the riverbank connector back to the road into Cragstone. The river reflects
the original bridge and nearby scenery around the crossing.

Spectators cheer, clap, and dance by the road. Drudges and Tuskers stage
duels in some stretches. Watch for pulsing amber rings. Their native fire,
frost and lightning effects briefly slow racers caught in the marked lane.
Each race rolls new roadside encounters. Tuskers toss barrels back and
forth across the road. Flashing Olthoi Crossing signs warn of a swarm
pouring across in groups. Lugian quarry crews chip boulders loose and
heave them across the road onto a growing pile. A flashing Rolling Death
sign warns of elemental spell projectiles moving along and across the
track. The spell uses the native acid, fire, frost and lightning effects
from Gaerlan's trials. Brake or steer through a gap. A clean hit can knock
anyone off their mount, and a Portal Ward can absorb a hit.

Each start rolls a new sky and lighting preset. Look for volumetric clouds,
low mist, showers, golden-hour light and moonlit nights with Dereth's two DAT
moons and a distant star field. Rain fills shallow depressions with reflective
puddles that drain after the shower. Terrain and native mount textures have
subtle surface relief and a matte finish.

The other 45 towns have Medium routes on 7-by-7 grids of 49 native landblocks.
Each grid covers 1,344 m by 1,344 m. The 4.9 to 5.8 km laps have 12 checkpoints
and a 24 m-wide road. Built-in roads follow the native slopes with packed dirt
and feathered gravel shoulders. Water crossings use local grading, and the
Holtburg sprint crosses on the original Obsidian Span. The DAT folder needs
both client_cell_1.dat and client_portal.dat. Native buildings and authored
scenery stay in place.

Steer past the edge to drive freely across open ground, then aim down the
road to rejoin. Cross the gold gates in order to complete a lap. Water,
steep terrain and solid scenery can block a shortcut. Medium layouts start
with fresh records. Times from retired layouts stay in the local save.
PICKUPS

Frostbolt   Leaves an icy ribbon as it curves toward a rival ahead. A hit slows them for just under three seconds.
Tusker Pus Lobs a homing glob at the closest rival. A hit recalls them to
           their latest checkpoint, with a short recovery period.
Portal Ward Absorbs the next hit for up to twelve seconds.
Bog Pepper Gives a burst of speed.
Mud Pie    Drops a slick behind you.
Battle Burp Slows nearby opponents.
White Rabbit Summons the Orb of the Bunny Booty and sends a rabbit after
             the next racer ahead, or second place if you're leading.
             The rabbit bites, then unseats the rival with Bunny Smite.
             The inset camera follows your rabbit and shows the impact in slow motion.
Gromnatross Lift  The flying mount carries your rider above the road.
                  Steer to bank during the lift.
Olthoi Lift       Press F to ride on the back of a native winged Olthoi Spitter.
                  Steer to bank, then return to your ground mount on landing.
Frost Volley      Three straight ice shots. Press F once per shot.
Pyreal Sling      Tosses five coins onto the road for anyone to collect.
Pyreal Cache      Three charges. Each adds a coin and a quick boost.
Smith's Hammer    Arcs into the road and briefly stays as an obstacle.
Leaping Draught   Jump over hazards while keeping control of your mount.
Zefir Hex         Briefly turns up to two rivals ahead into slower rabbits.
Tusker Might      Grow, gain speed, and knock back rivals on contact.
Invulnerability   Clear slowing, boost, and become immune to attacks.
Force Burst       Blasts nearby rivals, incoming shots, and road hazards.
Wisp Thief        Phase through attacks and steal the nearest held item.
Ulgrim's Folly    Splash a rival with Ulgrim's Casting Stein. Suds and
                  reversed steering, throttle, and brake last ten seconds.

Pyreals add a little top speed for the rest of the race, up to ten.
The item HUD shows remaining charges and your current Pyreal count.

Ride through the marked feather near the start of Yanshi XL or Holtburg XL
to take the longer flyover. Follow the wind rings and land before the next
checkpoint. Ground racing stays available across the full road width.

Frostbolt uses the native frost projectile and effect. The custom Tusker
Pus pickup uses the native acid projectile and effect, with a recall ring
at the checkpoint. The game opens the DATs read-only.

LAUNCH

On this PC the launcher detects the local Godot 4.7.2 .NET runtime and the
DAT folder at C:TurbineAsheron's Call. You can also select your DAT folder
in-game, set ASHERONS_CALL_DAT, or pass -DatFolder. To use a different Godot
installation, pass -Godot with its console .exe path or set GROMNIE_GODOT.
A .NET 8 or later SDK is required. Appearance, course, weapon and best
times are saved locally in the game's Godot user data folder.

Play.cmd -SmokeTest checks all XL grids, an entire XL lap, palettes, mounted weapons and terrain blending,
three-lap races on Holtburg and Muckwater, item hits and checkpoint recall,
both swipe sides, rider ejection, a dry off-track shortcut, ordered gates, and course switching.

Native file loaders, terrain blends and particle code carry over from the
local Dereth Survivors project. Town destination and weapon metadata comes
from that project's catalog of the ACE world data. Native models, textures
and animations are loaded at runtime from the local DATs.
