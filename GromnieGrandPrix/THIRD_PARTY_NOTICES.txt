OpenAC terrain blend and ACE packet checksum algorithms

Native terrain palette recipes and alpha-mask selection/rotation in
Native/AcTerrainBlend.cs, and region and terrain format support in
Native/AcWorldData.cs, are carried over from Dereth Survivors. Their adapted
OpenAC terrain algorithms follow AcDream.Core/Terrain/TerrainBlending.cs and
AcDream.Core/Rendering/Wb/TerrainUtils.cs.
Native/AceIsaacRandom.cs adapts AcDream.Core.Net/Cryptography/IsaacRandom.cs
for the stock ACE connection.
